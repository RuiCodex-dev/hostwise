Hostwise

Dinner for people who don't share a taste. Yet.

Rui Codex

Built on the Qloo Taste AI API for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon

Six colleagues. Six different tastes.
One table. And tonight,
you're the host.
Ask a chatbot,
and you'll get a crowd-pleasing film and some
pasta. Someone always spends the evening being polite.
Hostwise starts with what each guest actually loves:
a couple of films,
artists, shows or books.
Each one is matched in Qloo's taste graph,
and the names never leave your browser.
Then the agent plans.
It pools the whole table,
scores every option against each guest on their
own, and critiques its own pick.
Here, The Smiths would leave Ana and Leila
behind, so it re-plans from their side of
the graph.
You get seating built from what neighbours really
share, a menu inferred from cross-domain taste and
adapted to dietary needs,
a soundtrack in three moods,
and a film short enough for after dinner.
And every pick shows its evidence:
how well it fits each guest,
which favourites pulled it in,
and what a single pooled recommendation would have
missed.
Under the hood:
Qloo search, insights with explainability,
per-guest ranking, cuisine tag affinities and audience comparison.
About fifty calls per evening,
cached and rate-limited.
