## Title

Hostwise – an AI host that plans dinner for different tastes | Qloo Agentic Hackathon 2026

## Description

Hostwise is a hosting agent for mixed groups: new neighbours, a team offsite, meeting the in-laws. Give it two to four favourite films, artists, series, books, podcasts or games for each guest, and it plans the evening: who sits next to whom, a menu, a soundtrack in three moods, an after-dinner film and conversation cards.

How it uses Qloo's taste graph:
• Search matches each favourite to a Qloo entity.
• Insights with explainability pools the whole table and shows whose favourites pulled each candidate in.
• Per-guest ranking (filter.entities) scores every shortlisted option against each guest on their own.
• A least-misery critique flags anyone left out, and the agent re-plans from that guest's side of the graph.
• Cuisine tag affinities (place genres) turn film, music and book taste into a menu, adapted locally to dietary needs.
• Audience comparison between every pair of guests drives the seating and the conversation cards.
About 50 Qloo calls per evening, cached privately, paced and capped per day.

Privacy: guest names never leave the browser, dietary needs are never sent to Qloo, and the API key stays on the server.
