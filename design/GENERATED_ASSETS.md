# Generated assets log

Every AI-generated asset in this repository. Generated imagery is **mood only**: it never
shows product UI, people, or anything presented as a real photograph of a real customer,
user or event. No image contains model-rendered text; every word on screen is real
typography in HTML. All product UI in screenshots and the video is the real app.

## Images: Google Flow, Nano Banana Pro

- Tool: Google Flow via `gflow-cli` 0.80.0, `gflow image t2i --model nano-pro -n 3`, on the
  owner's Google AI Ultra account (Chrome profile `hesap5`, owner tag `qloo-visuals`).
- Date: 2026-10-15. Cost: 0 Flow credits (image generation). **No Veo video was generated.**
- 7 prompts x 3 variants = 21 images; one chosen per prompt by hand. Raw variants stay in
  `design/gen/raw/` (git-ignored, not published). Prompts: `design/prompts.json`; script:
  `design/generate.py`.
- Post-processing (`design/grade.py`): 0.6 % edge trim, aubergine shadow split-tone, warm
  highlights, saturation 0.93, gentle S-curve, seeded fine grain. PNG masters in
  `design/art/`, WebP sizes in `public/img/`.

Shared style suffix appended to every prompt:

> Editorial still-life photograph shot on medium-format film with a 80mm lens, Kodak Portra
> 400 colour, warm candlelight and the last blue light of dusk from a window, deep aubergine
> and warm ink shadows, terracotta, olive, brass and cream linen tones, matte natural
> textures, fine organic film grain, gentle low contrast, quiet and intimate, real
> materials, no people, no faces, no hands, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no
> words, no logos, no labels, no signage.

## Narration: Microsoft Edge neural TTS

- `edge-tts` 7.3.1, voice `en-US-EmmaMultilingualNeural`, rate -4 %, from
  `video/narration.json`; word timings drive the burned-in captions. Script:
  `video/make_audio.py`.

## Not generated by AI

- All app UI, the wordmark, the favicon (hand-written SVG), the seating chart and every
  diagram: hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG.
- The demo video's app footage: real screen capture of the running app (Chrome DevTools
  screencast) during a live run against the Qloo API.
- Music: synthesised from code in `video/make_audio.py` (no samples, no AI model).
- Dish library and all copy: written for this project.
- Entity artwork shown inside the app (posters, artist photos) is served by Qloo's image
  URLs at runtime and is not stored in this repository.
